Hangman

Category

6lives

How to play

Guess a hidden word one letter at a time before the drawing is complete.

  1. A category hint is shown above the gallows.
  2. Click a letter on the keyboard, or type on your keyboard.
  3. Correct letters appear in the word.
  4. Wrong letters cost one life and add a part to the drawing.
  5. You have six lives. Lose them all and the round is over.
  6. Guess every letter to win. New word starts a fresh round.
